# Quad pilot: one quadruped, two trainers

Phase 4 pilot of [docs/Plan-TrainingMethodComparison.md](../../docs/Plan-TrainingMethodComparison.md).
It uses the same pattern as [training/worm/WORM_SPEC.md](../worm/WORM_SPEC.md): one body, one
contract, 30 minutes each. The trainers are **plain MuJoCo Warp** (`training/quad/mujoco`)
and **Isaac Lab 3 + Newton, MuJoCo Warp solver** (`ISAAC/quad_tasks_v3`). Both race in Unity
on the **MuJoCo plug-in**.

Everything below is identical for both trainers. Where this file is silent, the worm's
"Details resolved" items 1–16 apply, with "segment 2" read as "torso".

## Body: `training/quad/quad.xml`

- Start from `training/bugs/Quad_v01.xml`: 9 bodies, 8 hinges (hip and knee per leg,
  pitch only), 90 kg, root at rest 0.90 m.
- **Force limit 300 N·m per joint** (the prefab has 900). About 7× the static knee load,
  so it can trot and jump like a large animal but not beyond (rule I; the worm showed
  that excess strength buys cheating gaits).
- **Friction 0.9**, floor and body, no bounce (AGENTS 2E). The bugs' 0.6 was only there
  to copy Unity's default PhysX material.
- **kp 1500 N·m/rad and joint damping 100 N·m·s/rad** (the prefab has 4500 and 300), scaled down by 3 with the force limit. At 4500 the servo hit the 300 N·m clamp after only 3.8° of error, so it acted as an on/off torque switch and the policy buzzed. At 1500, full force comes at 11°. Holding a 1 rad/s swing against damping 300 took the whole force limit; at 100 it takes a third. Armature and joint ranges stay as in `Quad_v01.xml`. Physics dt
  0.005 s; **policy 20 Hz (decimation 10)**. It was 50 Hz; Unity's own creature agent decides every 0.1 s, and 20 Hz removes the remaining action buzz.
- Generated by `training/quad/build_quad.py` from `Quad_v01.xml`, with a `quad_rig.json`
  as the single source of truth for Unity.

## Observation

In order: gravity in the torso frame (3), torso linear velocity (3, ×0.5), torso angular
velocity (3, ×0.25), joint positions (8, ÷ action scale), joint velocities (8, ×0.1),
previous action (8), goal direction in the torso frame, horizontal and normalised (2),
**target speed ÷ 2 (1)**. 36 floats. Action order is `Quad_v01_rig.json`'s `actuator_order`.
**Round 8 (MuJoCo side):** + the reference-gait clock (sin φ, cos φ) → **38 floats**; see "Round 8" below.

## Action

8 values, clipped to [−1, 1]. Joint target = rest + action × 0.785398 rad (45°).

## Task and reward (weights per second, × dt 0.05 at 20 Hz)

The goal is world +x. The target speed is the Froude-0.25 speed: v = √(0.25 · 9.81 · 0.90)
= **1.49 m/s**, and it is observed.

| Term | Weight | Formula |
|---|---|---|
| alive | **+1.0** | every policy step the quad has not fallen. Round 6: in round 5 every live step scored negative and a fall cost nothing, so the quad learned to fall on purpose |
| speed tracking | 2.0 | exp(−((v_x − 1.49) / **1.0**)²), **two-sided**. σ was 0.5, widened in round 6 so there is signal from standstill |
| progress | **1.0** (0.5 in round 4) | clip(v_x, 0, 1.49) ÷ 1.49. Added in round 4: pays for any forward movement, so standing still is not a trap |
| heading | **0.1** (was 0.3) | torso forward axis, projected and normalised · goal |
| upright | **0.2** (was 0.5) | torso up · world up |
| lateral drift | −0.5 | abs(torso world velocity · y) |
| effort | **−0.2** | mean over joints of (actuator force ÷ 300)². Was −0.02 |
| action rate | **−0.2** | mean over joints of (aₜ − aₜ₋₁)² per policy step. Was −0.02, then −0.5 |
| flight | **−1.0** | per second while no foot is down (debounced contact state). Round 7: at Froude 0.25 a quadruped walks or trots with almost no flight phase, but round 6 was airborne 57 % (MuJoCo) and patted the ground in 29 ms taps (Isaac) |
| vertical bounce | **−2.0** | (torso world v_z)², (m/s)². Added in round 3: stops the bounding gait |
| feet air time | **+0.5** | at each foot's debounced touchdown, add (min(t_air, 0.5 s) − 0.25 s), but **only while v_x > 0.3 m/s**; per footfall, not per second. Contact state changes only after ≥ 3 substeps (15 ms). Round 4 had no cap, no debounce and weight 1.0: the quad knelt on its thighs and farmed the term by waving its legs |
| foot slip | **−1.0** | Σ over lower legs in contact of the horizontal speed of the foot point (m/s). Was −0.1 |

**Why the three penalties were raised:** the first MuJoCo smoke run hit 1.47 m/s in two minutes with a 50 Hz buzz-hop: actions flipped between ±1 every step, no foot was on the ground 57 % of the time, feet slid at 1.04 m/s in contact, and some actuator sat at 300 N·m all the time (rule I). At the old weights that behaviour cost almost nothing next to the speed reward.

**Round 4** (after round 3's 20 ms foot taps on the MuJoCo side and a policy that stood still on the Isaac side): adds progress and feet air time, lowers upright, heading and action rate. Judge smokes on **4 minutes**, not 2.

## Round 8: reference trot — MuJoCo side validated (Isaac copies once proven)

All round-7 terms stay. Added (MuJoCo side only, `training/quad/mujoco/quad_env.py` Q16 and
`training/creature/env.py` `CreatureConfig.reference_gait`):

- **The prefab's coded gait is not usable as the reference.** Replayed open-loop on this body
  (kp 1500, damping 100, 300 N·m, 20 Hz, friction 0.9, 20 s) at 1.0 / 1.5 / 2.0 Hz with
  amplitude ×1.0 / 0.7 / 0.5, it goes nowhere (−0.02 to +0.02 m/s in all 9 variants). Its knee
  sine flexes equally both ways a quarter period after the hip, so the foot lifts twice per
  cycle and ground contact is not tied to the hip phase (stance probability 0.23 vs 0.23 for
  hip sine < 0 vs ≥ 0).
- **Reference used instead:** the prefab's diagonal trot pairs (hip phases 0, π, π, 0 for RL,
  FL, RR, FR), hip = 0.3 × sin(φ + ph), knee = 0.5 × max(0, −cos(φ + ph)) (a one-sided lift during
  the forward swing), both × 0.785398 rad, **f = 1.5 Hz**. Open-loop it walks 0.39 m/s with no
  falls, 15 % flight, slip 0.40 m/s, 13 % saturation, and its stance is tied to the phase
  (stance probability 0.63 when −cos < 0, 0.10 otherwise). Its real stances are 0.20–0.25 s;
  the short "stances" in the statistics are toe scuffs during the swing.
- **Clock:** per-world φ ~ U(0, 2π) at reset, φ += 2π · 1.5 · 0.05 after the physics of each
  policy step; the observation gets (sin φ, cos φ) at indices 36–37.
- **gait reference** +2.0 per second × exp(−mean over the 8 joints of (q − q_ref)² / 0.3²).
- **contact phase** +0.5 per second × the share of the 4 feet whose debounced contact state
  equals "stance expected" (−cos(φ + ph_hip) < 0).
- No annealing yet.

**Round 8 smoke (MuJoCo, 4 min, 2048 envs, 21 M steps), 10 × 20 s deterministic evaluation:**
1.33 m/s, 0 % falls (0.1 % in training at the end), flight 28.5 %, 50-Hz-equivalent action rate
0.057, slip 0.47 m/s, debounced stance 0.061 s per footfall but 0.131 s time-weighted with 45 %
of stance time in stances ≥ 0.15 s, peak foot impact 15 body weights, gait-reference term 0.73,
contact-phase term 0.64. **Stop rule: still fails on slip, stance and impacts.**

**Foot compliance (measured, not applied):** giving only the lower-leg geoms a softer contact
(solref time constant, with `priority` so the foot's value wins over the floor's): spawn-drop
peak 4.3 → 2.0 → 1.4 body weights for 0.01 → 0.02 → 0.03 s, reference-gait peak 5.6 → 2.7 → 2.2,
stand height 0.8999 → 0.8995 → 0.8992 m. At 0.03 s the reference gait's share of stance time in
stances ≥ 0.15 s rises from 65 % to 75 % (debounced; raw contact 22 % → 70 %). With rigid 0.01 s feet the ≤ 4 body-weight target is
below what even a 2 cm drop produces.

## Round 9: soft feet, stronger reference, new stop rule (MuJoCo side)

- **Body: soft ground.** The **floor** geom gets solref (0.03, 1) (a paw-pad-like contact, 3× the
  rigid 0.01) and **priority 1**, so every floor contact uses the floor's solref, solimp and
  friction. All body geoms stay at solref 0.01 / priority 0, so leg-on-leg contacts stay rigid.
  `quad_rig.json` has a top-level `floor` block (solref, solimp, priority, friction) because Unity
  builds its own floor, and lists solref, solimp, solmix, priority and margin for every geom.
  Checks (CPU MuJoCo): 2 cm spawn drop peaks at **1.334 body weights** on one foot (4.3 with the
  rigid floor), stand height 0.8992 m (0.66 mm lower; steady foot sink 0.83 mm, 7 mm for a moment
  after the drop), leg-on-leg overlap under random actions 19 mm (rigid, as before).
  Rejected on the way: soft feet (lower legs at priority 1; soft against each other too, legs
  overlapped 40–65 mm) and explicit floor/foot `<pair>`s (do not pass through USD or the Unity
  builder, and the randomised friction would not reach the feet).
- **Randomisation change:** because the floor supplies the friction of every floor contact, the
  per-episode friction factor U(0.85, 1.15) now scales **the floor geom's** friction only
  (0.9 × U); body geoms stay at 0.9.
- **Reward:** contact phase **2.0** per second (was 0.5); gait reference σ **0.2 rad** (was 0.3).
  Everything else as round 8.
- **Stop rule for the 30-minute run** (all must hold, 10 × 20 s deterministic evaluation):
  speed ≥ 1.0 m/s (4-minute smoke), falls ≤ 5 %, flight (debounced, all feet up) ≤ 40 %, foot slip
  in stance ≤ 0.3 m/s, peak foot impact ≤ ~4 body weights (after the first 0.5 s), 50-Hz-equivalent
  action rate ≤ 0.1, and — replacing "stance per footfall ≥ 0.15 s", which counts toe scuffs —
  **debounced duty factor ≥ 0.35 and time-weighted stance (Σd² ÷ Σd over debounced stances) ≥ 0.15 s**.

## Episodes

20 s = 400 policy steps at 20 Hz. **Stage 1 (this pilot): a fall ends the episode as terminal**. A fall is **the torso or any upper leg touching the floor** (round 5: catches kneeling and sitting; round 4 knelt on its front thighs at 0.55 m, which the old rule missed), or torso
up · world up < 0.5, or torso height < 0.45 m (50 % of stance). Timeouts bootstrap. The
health guard (worm item 12) applies. The get-up stage comes later (plan step 5).

Reset: the rest pose at 0.90 m + 2 cm, yaw uniform ±45°, joint noise ±0.05 rad, zero
velocity. Randomised per reset: friction ×U(0.85, 1.15), body masses ×U(0.9, 1.1),
kp ×U(0.8, 1.2), plus a 0.5 m/s push in a random horizontal direction every 10–15 s. From round 9 the friction factor
applies to the floor only (it has contact priority; body geoms stay 0.9).

## PPO and budget

As the worm: 3×128 ELU, initial std 0.5, 4096 envs × 24 steps, 5 epochs, 4 minibatches,
lr 3e-4 at most, **adaptive-KL schedule with a target KL of 0.01** (it only lowers the lr; round 4). Isaac3Worm's collapse was a constant lr that was too large once the action noise had shrunk, per-update KL up to 0.15, γ 0.99, λ 0.95, clip 0.2, entropy 0.005. **30 minutes each**, one after
the other, Unity closed, TensorBoard first. Keep a checkpoint every 50 iterations. If a run
collapses (Isaac3Worm did), report both the final checkpoint and the best one before the
collapse.
