# Quad pilot: one quadruped, two trainers

Phase 4 pilot of [docs/Plan-TrainingMethodComparison.md](../../docs/Plan-TrainingMethodComparison.md).
It uses the same pattern as [training/worm/WORM_SPEC.md](../worm/WORM_SPEC.md): one body, one
contract, 30 minutes each. The trainers are **plain MuJoCo Warp** (`training/quad/mujoco`)
and **Isaac Lab 3 + Newton, MuJoCo Warp solver** (`ISAAC/quad_tasks_v3`). Both race in Unity
on the **MuJoCo plug-in**.

Everything below is identical for both trainers. Where this file is silent, the worm's
"Details resolved" items 1–16 apply, with "segment 2" read as "torso".

## Body: `training/quad/quad.xml`

- Start from `training/bugs/Quad_v01.xml`: 9 bodies, 8 hinges (hip and knee per leg,
  pitch only), 90 kg, root at rest 0.90 m.
- **Force limit 300 N·m per joint** (the prefab has 900). About 7× the static knee load,
  so it can trot and jump like a large animal but not beyond (rule I; the worm showed
  that excess strength buys cheating gaits).
- **Friction 0.9**, floor and body, no bounce (AGENTS 2E). The bugs' 0.6 was only there
  to copy Unity's default PhysX material.
- Joint damping, armature, kp and joint ranges stay as in `Quad_v01.xml`. Physics dt
  0.005 s; policy 50 Hz (decimation 4).
- Generated by `training/quad/build_quad.py` from `Quad_v01.xml`, with a `quad_rig.json`
  as the single source of truth for Unity.

## Observation

In order: gravity in the torso frame (3), torso linear velocity (3, ×0.5), torso angular
velocity (3, ×0.25), joint positions (8, ÷ action scale), joint velocities (8, ×0.1),
previous action (8), goal direction in the torso frame, horizontal and normalised (2),
**target speed ÷ 2 (1)**. 36 floats. Action order is `Quad_v01_rig.json`'s `actuator_order`.

## Action

8 values, clipped to [−1, 1]. Joint target = rest + action × 0.785398 rad (45°).

## Task and reward (weights per second, × dt 0.02)

The goal is world +x. The target speed is the Froude-0.25 speed: v = √(0.25 · 9.81 · 0.90)
= **1.49 m/s**, and it is observed.

| Term | Weight | Formula |
|---|---|---|
| speed tracking | 2.0 | exp(−((v_x − 1.49) / 0.5)²), **two-sided** |
| heading | 0.3 | torso forward axis, projected and normalised · goal |
| upright | 0.5 | torso up · world up |
| lateral drift | −0.5 | abs(torso world velocity · y) |
| effort | −0.02 | mean over joints of (actuator force ÷ 300)² |
| action rate | −0.02 | mean over joints of (aₜ − aₜ₋₁)² |
| foot slip | −0.1 | Σ over lower legs in contact of horizontal speed (m/s) |

## Episodes

20 s. **Stage 1 (this pilot): a fall ends the episode as terminal**. A fall is torso
up · world up < 0.5, or torso height < 0.45 m (50 % of stance). Timeouts bootstrap. The
health guard (worm item 12) applies. The get-up stage comes later (plan step 5).

Reset: the rest pose at 0.90 m + 2 cm, yaw uniform ±45°, joint noise ±0.05 rad, zero
velocity. Randomised per reset: friction ×U(0.85, 1.15), body masses ×U(0.9, 1.1),
kp ×U(0.8, 1.2), plus a 0.5 m/s push in a random horizontal direction every 10–15 s.

## PPO and budget

As the worm: 3×128 ELU, initial std 0.5, 4096 envs × 24 steps, 5 epochs, 4 minibatches,
lr 3e-4 constant, γ 0.99, λ 0.95, clip 0.2, entropy 0.005. **30 minutes each**, one after
the other, Unity closed, TensorBoard first. Keep a checkpoint every 50 iterations. If a run
collapses (Isaac3Worm did), report both the final checkpoint and the best one before the
collapse.

## Export and tests

ONNX `obs[1,36]` → `actions[1,8]`, normaliser baked in: `training/quad/export/quad_mujoco.onnx`
and `quad_isaaclab3.onnx`, each with a report.
- 100 × 20 s evaluation: speed, distance, fall rate, and worm-style realism metrics.
- Cross-check each brain in plain MuJoCo.

- Run the walking exam (W1–W4, W7).
