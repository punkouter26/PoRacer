Scenes Layout & Object Reference

Every scene in Assets/Scenes/, every authored GameObject, what each one does, and what MessagePipe messages it publishes or subscribes to.

Three progressive detail tiers — Tier 1 (Executive, <30 s) · Tier 2 (Component & Flow) · Tier 3 (Per-object Reference).

Tier 1 · Executive summary

6 scenes · 1 runtime boot, 4 training, 1 race

Boot: SCN_RACE_FLAT is the only runtime scene — it auto-loads on launch and runs the endless race loop. Training: SCN_TRAIN_WORM, SCN_TRAIN_SPIDER, SCN_TRAIN_ALL, SCN_TRAIN_WORM_ROUGH contain ≥ 8 parallel training areas and are launched only inside the headless Builds/WormEnv and Builds/AllEnv player builds via mlagents-learn.

Tier 2 · Component & flow

How a runtime launch wires up

  1. GameLifetimeScope (Assets/Scripts/) registers VContainer: RaceModel, EloModel, CommentaryModel, RaceConfigModel, CreatureCatalog, TrackCatalog, plus all Systems_* as singletons.
  2. Systems_AppBootstrap on the root GameObject of SCN_RACE_FLAT hooks MessagePipe, fires RaceConfigModel.NotifyChanged(), and shows the menu UI.
  3. The menu (MenuView) edits RaceConfigModel counts; tapping Start Race calls Systems_Spawn.BeginRacing() which instantiates every selected creature prefab, hooks BehaviorParameters to its .onnx model, and publishes RaceStartedMessage.
  4. Systems_Race tracks each racer's progress and finish order. Systems_CameraDirector switches camera targets on RaceStartedMessage and CheckpointPassedMessage. Systems_Elo subscribes RaceFinishedMessage; Systems_Persistence writes elo.json + race-history.json.
  5. Training scenes have no race HUD — they host Systems_TrainingArea on each area, which resets the agent + goal and reads curriculum knobs from Academy.EnvironmentParameters.

What this scene does

The only scene loaded at runtime. On launch, the menu shows up via Systems_AppBootstrap.Start. The user picks per-creature counts (0 / 1 / 10 / 50 / 100), selects a map (Flat / Lumpy / Swamp), optionally toggles scripted brains, and taps Start Race.

Systems_Spawn instantiates the chosen creatures in a 10-wide grid behind the start line, hooks each to its .onnx model (BehaviorType.InferenceOnly, InferenceDevice.Burst), and tints it with a per-racer HSV step (golden ratio) so a 100-racer race stays readable.

Racers race until the last crosses the finish line or the 120 s race timeout. Systems_Race reports finishing order, including same-frame ties resolved by overshoot. Systems_Elo updates ratings; Systems_Persistence writes JSON. After a 5 s pause, the next race begins.

2. Training Scenes

Training Assets/Scenes/SCN_TRAIN_WORM.unity

SCN_TRAIN_WORM — Worm-only, flat ground

├── ▶ TrainingArea × 8+ (≥ 8 parallel, per project rule) │ ├── Agent_Creature (Worm prefab, MaxStep = 3000) │ ├── BehaviorParameters (Default, attached to trainer) │ ├── DecisionRequester (DecisionStep = 5) │ ├── Systems_TrainingArea — resets worm, randomizes goal │ ├── Goal (Transform) │ ├── SpawnPoint (Transform) │ └── TrackRoot — rebuilt per episode (obstacles / kind) │ ├── ▶ Academy │ └── Academy + Brain (Worm, PPO 256×2) │ └── ▶ Track builder └── Systems_TrackBuilder — built per episode via curriculum

Purpose

P1 slice: prove the training pipeline end-to-end. Systems_TrainingArea.Awake caches the authored stiffness / forceLimit per body so per-episode quirk scaling (from quirk_power_span) never compounds across resets.

Curriculum knobs read

goal_distance_max (3–20 m), rough_amplitude (0..1), track_kind (Flat=0 default), quirk_power_span (0).

Training Assets/Scenes/SCN_TRAIN_SPIDER.unity

SCN_TRAIN_SPIDER — Spider-only

├── ▶ TrainingArea × 8+ │ ├── Agent_Creature (Spider prefab) │ └── Systems_TrainingArea with _trackKind = Flat │ ├── ▶ Academy │ └── Brain (Spider, PPO 256×2) │ └── ▶ Track builder └── Systems_TrackBuilder

Purpose

P3 second-morphology. Single-behavior Spider run uses SpiderLoco02.yaml. Same shared Agent_Creature, same Reward_WormLoco (named for history; reused across morphologies).

Training Assets/Scenes/SCN_TRAIN_ALL.unity

SCN_TRAIN_ALL — all nine creatures, mixed areas

├── ▶ TrainingArea (Worm) × 2 (pacing behavior) ├── ▶ TrainingArea (Spider) × 1 ├── ▶ TrainingArea (Hexapod) × 1 ├── ▶ TrainingArea (Quad) × 1 ├── ▶ TrainingArea (Snake) × 1 ├── ▶ TrainingArea (Centipede) × 1 ├── ▶ TrainingArea (Crab) × 1 ├── ▶ TrainingArea (Kangaroo) × 1 ├── ▶ TrainingArea (Blob) × 1 │ ├── ▶ Academy │ └── Brain × 9 (one per creature, PPO 256×2) │ └── ▶ Demonstration recorders └── DemoRecorder — emits .demo files into Assets/Demonstrations/<Creature>.demo (used by AllLoco03.yaml's BC + GAIL warm start).

Purpose

Bulk all-creatures training driven by AllLoco* YAMLs. The pacing behavior is Worm; curriculum in every AllLoco* YAML measures Worm's mean reward against thresholds (12/22 in gen-3 configs) before unlocking the next lesson.

Opt-in: Enable Demo Recorders

Menu PoRacer/Training/Enable Demo Recorders In Open Scene attaches DemoRecorder components so you can play a few minutes of Heuristic-only gaits and produce .demo files for the AllLoco03 BC + GAIL warm start.

Training Assets/Scenes/SCN_TRAIN_WORM_ROUGH.unity

SCN_TRAIN_WORM_ROUGH — rough terrain ablation

├── ▶ TrainingArea × 8+ │ ├── Agent_Creature (Worm prefab) │ ├── Systems_TrainingArea with _trackKind = Rough │ └── TrackRoot — Systems_TrackBuilder rebuilds │ terrain mesh per episode (Perlin 2-octave, 0.8 m amplitude) │ └── ▶ Academy └── Brain (Worm, PPO 256×2)

Purpose

Tests the worm against the Perlin rough terrain curriculum driven by rough_amplitude. Optional _useTerrainProbes on the agent adds 4 look-ahead height observations, requiring a matching VectorObservationSize bump and a retrain.

Terrain-probe retrain warning: flipping _useTerrainProbes changes the observation size; any previously trained .onnx brain becomes shape-incompatible and must be retrained from scratch.

├── ▶ CreatureRoot (the prefab root) │ ├── ArticulationBody (root, immutable per-episode) │ ├── ArticulationBody[] (joints; one per DoF) │ │ └── each carries an ArticulationDrive │ │ (target, stiffness, damping, forceLimit) │ ├── Agent_Creature (ML-Agents Agent + ICreatureAgent) │ ├── BehaviorParameters (BehaviorName = creature id) │ └── DecisionRequester (DecisionStep = 5 by default) │ └── ▶ Visual children ├── MeshRenderer per body part ├── MeshCollider per body part (where needed) └── ▶ Optional ├── BodyLinkView — added at runtime in race ├── DustTrailView — added at runtime in race └── CreatureAudioView — added at runtime in race
Per-creature details: each creature prefab (Worm, Spider, …) differs only in its ArticulationBody hierarchy, joint count, drive limits, and the per-joint gait tables (_gaitFrequency, _gaitPhases, _gaitAmplitudes, _gaitOffsets) used by Heuristic(). The shared Agent_Creature reads all of them.
