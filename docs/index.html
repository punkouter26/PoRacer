What the documentation says that the old edition got wrong

The previous suite carried a number of figures that do not survive contact with the files. They are corrected throughout, and called out where it matters:

The five things worth knowing before you touch anything

  • Δt = 0.005 s is load-bearing. It divides evenly into 0.02 s (IsaacH1, IsaacBox, Fido) and 0.025 s (MujocoBiped). Change it and you must change DecisionPeriod on all twelve ML-Agents prefabs and Fido's actionDecimation with it.
  • Promote on ELO, never on mean reward. The locomotion reward is potential-based, so its episode sum scales with survival time. A brain that stands still longer scores higher.
  • Colour is a legend. Red is reserved for heuristic bots, green for the baseline RL policy. A creature that arrived with authored art — IsaacBox does — keeps it and must not be recoloured.
  • Some bugs are load-bearing. MujocoBiped's double-rotated angular velocity and the TensorProxy finaliser noise are both documented as do not fix. Read the reason before touching either.
  • Commit to master. No feature branches unless explicitly asked, and verify before committing — compile clean, plus a play-mode check for anything touching runtime.
Two open high-severity items as of this regeneration. (1) Four creatures — Worm, Spider, Centipede and Crab — do not reach the finish on the 22 m Flat map. (2) Every brain trained between 2026-08-29 and 2026-09-02 learned against a distorted reward: the step-scaling fix landed on 09-02, and until then episodes were cut at 5 s of no progress instead of 20, with every per-step penalty landing four times per simulated second. Full list in the dashboard's gaps section.
